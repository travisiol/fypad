# FYPAD — three posts (< 280 chars each)

1. What it is
Every coin gets a TikTok.
Launch a coin on FYPAD, connect a TikTok account for it, and it gets short AI videos on a schedule: meme skit, hype edit or talking presenter. You approve each one before it posts. [link]

2. What people gain
Your coin's creator fees pay for its own videos. You pick the share (30–80 %), keep the rest of yours, and 20 % buys and burns $TICKER. Every transfer is on a public ledger. Coins are risky: you can lose what you put in. [link]

3. How it works
1. Launch: name, ticker, image, one line on the video style.
2. Trades pay creator fees into the coin's content budget.
3. When it covers a video, one is made; you approve, it posts to TikTok (private until TikTok audits the app). [link]
